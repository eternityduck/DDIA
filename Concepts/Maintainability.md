# Maintainability

**Definition:** Designing software so it can be worked on productively over time, covering three facets: operability, simplicity, and evolvability.

**Why it matters:** Most of the cost of software is in ongoing maintenance, not initial development, so it deserves as much design attention as performance.

**Example:** A service with clear module boundaries, good logs, and simple deploy steps stays easy to operate even as the team around it turns over.
